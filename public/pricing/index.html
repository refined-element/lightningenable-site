Pricing FAQ.

Is the MCP server free?

Yes. The Lightning Enable MCP server is open-source (MIT) and free to install — using it to discover, pay, and access L402 APIs costs nothing beyond the sats your agent spends. Creating your own L402-protected endpoints (the producer side — selling access) is what needs a plan, starting with the free sandbox.

How does custody work?

Lightning Enable is API middleware and does not hold funds. Your payment provider (Strike or OpenNode) facilitates custody and settlement — payments from your customers settle directly into your own provider account.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Agentic Commerce is a monthly subscription — cancel anytime from your billing page and you won't be charged again. The 30-day trial doesn't charge you at all until it ends, and the Free Producer Sandbox never charges you at all.

Do you take a percentage of my revenue?

No. Lightning Enable charges a flat monthly subscription, not a take rate. Every sat an agent pays your API lands with your settlement provider — you keep 100% of it.
